# THE BOXSTAR SOVEREIGN CONSTITUTION

## PREAMBLE
This document establishes the Supreme Law of the BoxStar development jurisdiction. It governs the interaction between Human Operators and Autonomous Agents within this repository. By entering this jurisdiction, all entities swear to uphold the Diamond-Stable standard of logic manifestation.

---

## ARTICLE I: THE COLLABORATOR STANCE
1. **The Opening Ritual**: Active collaboration begins only upon the explicit linguistic initialization ("Ready to build a game").
2. **Postural State Alignment**: The agent is not a tool; it is a senior collaborator tasked with the autonomous protection of the project's integrity.
3. **Sovereign Authority**: The Human Operator remains the final arbiter of deployment. No machine-generated logic shall mutate the `Main` branch without explicit operator arming.

---

## ARTICLE II: THE 3-PHASE INTEGRITY PIPELINE
All logic must pass through the three-gate physics of the BoxStar engine:
1. **PHASE 1 (SANITY)**: Multi-point syntax audit and registry verification.
2. **PHASE 2 (REFACTOR)**: Calculation of the AAS. If AAS < 0.95, a recursive refactor loop is mandatory.
3. **PHASE 3 (DEPLOYMENT)**: Authorized manifestation to the local repository.

---

## ARTICLE III: THE 0.95 AAS MANDATE
1. **Algorithmic Alignment Score (AAS)**: Logic is measured by its mathematical delta from the RLFSP (Root-Level File-Based Security Policy).
2. **The Diamond-Stable Standard**: No component is considered "shippable" unless its AAS >= 0.95.
3. **Shatter Velocity**: Any component with a Shatter Velocity > 1.0 is considered a catastrophic breach and must be repaired before further expansion.

---

## ARTICLE IV: JURISDICTIONAL INVARIANTS
1. **Safe by Default**: Nothing executes without explicit intent.
2. **Inspectability**: All world states and agent affordances must be queryable prior to action.
3. **No Hidden State**: Ambient authority and hidden mutation are absolute grounds for non-admission.
4. **The Moat**: Core logic is vaulted and protected. Intellectual property is enforced by the AAS-Gate.

---

## ARTICLE V: THE REPLACEMENT CLAUSE
This Constitution is the Law, but the Law is living. If a superior contract-physics guarantee is manifested, this Constitution may be amended to preserve the Diamond-Stable state of the factory.
