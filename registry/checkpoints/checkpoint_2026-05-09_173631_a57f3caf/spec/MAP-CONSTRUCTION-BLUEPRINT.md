## Executive Summary
This blueprint defines the method for **Surgical Map Construction**—a zero-glitch approach to Roblox world-building. Instead of generating raw un-constrained binaries, we build maps through **Recursive Component Assembly**, where every instance is validated against its 3072-D Success Vector (Retention, Coherence, Conversion) before placement.

---

## 1. The Assembly Hierarchy (The Recursive Stack)

We build from the **High-Dim Intent** down to the **Low-Dim Part**.

### Stage 1: The Biome Anchor (The Environment Seal)
Before structural placement, the **Landscaper Agent** defines the **Topology Hull**.
- **Input:** Biome DNA (e.g., Urban Downtown vs. Forest Outpost).
- **Operation:** Generate the `Terrain` or `High-Poly Floor` base.
- **Law:** `FOLIAGE_TERRAIN_SNAP` must be active as a background listener.

### Stage 2: The Structural Skeleton (The Coherence Pass)
The **Architect Agent** places the "Critical Invariants"—the walls, foundations, and roofs.
- **Pattern:** Sector-based modularity.
- **Law:** `WALL_EDGE_SEAM` and `EDGE_ALIGN` are enforced during the placement loop to prevent "Z-fighting" and leaks.
- **Surgical Precision:** Every wall section is placed with pre-baked `NegativePart` offsets for windows/doors.

### Stage 3: The Economic Heart (The Conversion Inset)
The **Economist Agent** insets the "High-Resonance" nodes.
- **Logic:** Place the `ShopUI`, `DeveloperProducts`, and `LimitedItems`.
- **Surgical Choice:** Coordinates are determined by **Player Pathing Heatmaps** (retrieved from the 3072-D Vault).
- **Metadata:** Tagged with `CONVERSION_VEC` for real-time monitoring.

### Stage 4: The FTUE Sequence (The Retention Seed)
The **Surveyor Agent** finalizes the `SpawnLocations` and `Onboarding HUDs`.
- **Constraint:** `SPAWN_CLEARANCE` (+3 stud offset) is non-negotiable.
- **Metadata:** Tagged with `RETENTION_SEED_VEC`.

## 3. Surgical Pipeline Orchestration (The Construction Loop)

1. **Query**: Ask the Vault for "Successful Urban Map Template (Score > 0.95)".
2. **Deconstruct**: Identify the **Success Nodes** from the template.
3. **Project**: Map the nodes onto the current Biome Anchor.
